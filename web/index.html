Automated Evaluation & Reliability Engineering

Exposing RAG Hallucinations Through Controlled Systematic Stress

Standard RAG benchmarks assume single-document relevance, zero distractor noise, and cooperative sources. In production, enterprise retrieval surfaces conflicting drafts, outdated policies, and 50+ noise passages. RAG-Stress quantifies exactly where and why retrieval-augmented systems break.

0% Pre-Training Leakage

Built over a seed-deterministic synthetic KB of 150 entities and 600 typed facts using pronounceable pseudo-words that cannot exist in foundation model pre-training.

Deterministic Grading

Zero LLM-as-a-judge. All answers, temporal conflicts, multi-document citations, and abstentions are verified via exact structured normalizers.

Paired Statistical Power

Identical query items act as their own controls, evaluated with McNemar's exact test and 95% bootstrap difference intervals to eliminate question selection variance.

End-to-End Evaluation Architecture

Six decoupled, byte-reproducible phases executing from synthetic generation to paired hypothesis testing.
PHASE 01
Synthetic KB
150 entities, 600 facts, 300 passages. Byte-identical seed determinism with collision-proof attributes.
PHASE 02
Retrieval Stack
Pure-Python BM25, deterministic hash embedder, flat vector store, and reciprocal rank fusion (RRF).
PHASE 03
Stress Engine
Injects near-miss distractors, temporal/authority conflict twins, opaque doc IDs, and positional permutations.
PHASE 04
Structured Replay
Forced JSON tool-calls with SHA-256 content-addressed cassette recording for 100% offline regression CI.
PHASE 05
Graders & Taxonomy
Normalizes currencies, dates, and durations. Classifies errors into 8 mutually exclusive taxonomy bins.
PHASE 06
Paired Analytics
Wilson score intervals, paired McNemar exact tests, and Lost-in-the-Middle positional penalty scores.
